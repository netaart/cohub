Add custom domains for published Apps.

- SDK: `apps.listDomains()`, `addDomain()`, `checkDomain()`, `removeDomain()`, the `AppDomainRecord` types, and `app.domain.updated` / `app.domain.removed` space events.
- CLI: `cohub apps domains ls|add|check|rm`. A binding costs a one-time $5 when it goes live; `add` asks for confirmation unless `--yes` is passed.
