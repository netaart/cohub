Bring back replies: `space.prompt({ reply: "note" | "prompt" })` and `cohub spaces prompt --reply` deliver the final result of a prompt sent from a Cohub Turn back to the calling Chat, and `prompt` runs its Agent again. The `reply` origin kind, `SentTurnRef.reply`, `readSessionTurnReply()` and `readReplyStatus()` return. Prompts that open with a mention of another Space still run here.
