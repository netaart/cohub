Upload large files in parts with bounded retries and resume failed transfers within the current client session. Share the upload task across public assets, workspace files, public files, and CLI uploads while preserving existing upload protocols.

Allow client defaults and per-upload overrides for multipart mode, threshold, part size, shared batch concurrency, attempt count, and request timeout. Upload groups keep one validated configuration for preparation, transfer, and completion.
