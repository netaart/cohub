`cohub boards examples` is now five starters, each teaching one kind of Board: `diagram` (was `basic` and `workflow`), `slides` (was `slides` and `lesson`), `custom` (now the sparks script, with params, a hit region and shared effects), `rain` and `arena`; `tilt`, `senses`, `sparks` and `depth` are gone. Unknown examples fail with a clean error. Board help groups the commands by task, and `sequence`, `path`, `schema`, `history` and `play` gain usage examples. The SDK drops `BOARD_SCRIPT_DRAWING_EXAMPLE`, `BOARD_SCRIPT_SENSES_EXAMPLE` and `BOARD_SCRIPT_TILT_EXAMPLE`; `BOARD_SCRIPT_SPARKS_EXAMPLE` declares a `count` parameter.
