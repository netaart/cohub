Space details, the SDK, CLI details, and Space settings show cached cloud workspace storage measurements. A pdu system job refreshes changed or running workspaces, leaves unchanged stopped spaces alone, and marks a workspace dirty before and after a direct write.

Space 详情、SDK、CLI 详情和设置页可以查看云端工作区占用的缓存值。后台 pdu 系统任务按需更新变化或运行中的空间，跳过已统计且未变化的关机空间，并在直接写入前后标记工作区需要重新统计。
