Local Runtimes without a Harness: `cohub runtime up` now serves the folder when no local Harness can run, with Turns on the Cohub Harness. The host service reports why a connection failed, refuses to disconnect over unconfirmed work unless forced, stops an account's Runtimes at sign-out, shares a screen without restarting the Runtime, and keeps the machine awake during tasks. The host bridge adds permissions, the app badge, launch at login, a global shortcut, incoming shares and `runtime.keepAwake`.
