Calls client.auth.authorize() with a Space or account target. Reuses a previous grant silently and reports the actual target; the consent dialog opens only when something new is needed.

Pick a Space

Calls auth.authorize({ target: { kind: 'pick-space' } }). One consent merges the choice and the grant; the host loads the Space list and the result names the Space the app may use.
